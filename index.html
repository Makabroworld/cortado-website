A free video editor for Windows

Cortado is a desktop video editor built around plain-English prompts. Describe the edit you want and it happens; drop into a real multi-track timeline whenever you want manual control.

Prompt-to-edit

Type what you want changed — cut silences, burn in captions, resize to 9:16 — and preview the result before you keep it.

Full timeline editor

Multi-track video and audio, trimming, transitions, effects, filters, text, and keyframed transforms for when a prompt isn't precise enough.

Automatic captions

On-device speech recognition turns your footage into word-level, editable captions with full control over font, colour, and animation.

Local rendering

Your footage is decoded, edited, and exported on your own machine — video files are not uploaded anywhere to be processed.

Frequently asked questions

Is Cortado free?

Yes. Every editing feature works on the free tier, including automatic subtitles and unlimited local exports. An optional Pro subscription raises the daily allowance for AI-powered features.

What platforms does Cortado support?

Cortado is currently available for Windows 10 and later.

Does Cortado upload my videos?

No. Your footage is decoded, edited, and exported locally on your own machine — video files are not sent anywhere for processing.

How do I install it?

Click Download for Windows above, run the installer, and follow the setup wizard.
